Plate 12 · Foundation

Shelves, not folders

Organize work as flexible shelves, not folders—membership changes without moving or deleting the underlying entity.

What the plate verifies

Shipped capability

Collections gather heterogeneous entities through membership edges; they can subdivide or curate other collections without moving or copying members.

How the proof works

Interactive evidence

Eight-frame Play / Step / scrub shelf assembly; remove/restore membership demonstration and an expandable nesting explanation.

Exact canonical registry copy

Plate lede

A collection gathers different kinds without moving or copying them. Drive the press once: assemble a shelf, slide the kind lens across it, then remove one membership without touching the entity.

Evidence boundary

Say this carefully

A collection cannot contain itself; re-adding repositions its one membership edge rather than duplicating it.
