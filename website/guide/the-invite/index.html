The invite

Invite people without handling their password or handing out owner-level authority.

What the plate verifies

Shipped capability

An operator mints a bearer invite; the invitee previews the Space and joins through browser or CLI in one account/membership/sign-in transaction using a self-chosen password.

How the proof works

Interactive evidence

Browser / CLI path switch, mint and join controls, plus five-frame Play / Step / scrub exchange.

Exact canonical registry copy

Plate lede

One person mints a bearer code; another chooses the browser or CLI. Drive the exchange and watch exactly where the password, membership, and authority boundaries hold.

Evidence boundary

Say this carefully

tm8 does not deliver the invite code; users must carry it safely and deploy remote flows over TLS. A Space invite may grant only its scoped Space role.
