A teammate, equipped

Compose who works with how they work by equipping reusable skills on durable teammates.

What the plate verifies

Shipped capability

equips attaches skills to a durable teammate and resolves them into the next session launch manifest; ordinary attachments remain context, not skills.

How the proof works

Interactive evidence

Plate lede

A teammate is the durable identity. Its equipped skills become instructions in the next session’s launch manifest—so you can compose who works with how they work.

Evidence boundary

Say this carefully

The preview writes nothing. Instructions are not authority; equipping does not change access or tools. Spell injection/casting is explicitly not wired.
